# Bharat Enterprise — Subscription Plans & Pricing

> Commercial tiers, feature entitlement matrix, duration discounts, and enterprise limits.

Bharat Enterprise offers straightforward, tiered SaaS plans tailored for different operational scales.

---

## 2. Billing Durations & Volume Discounts

Tenants can subscribe across flexible billing horizons with compounding discounts:
* **Monthly**: Standard flexible billing for emerging businesses.
* **Quarterly (3 Months)**: 5% duration incentive.
* **Semi-Annual (6 Months)**: 10% duration incentive.
* **Annual (12 Months)**: Best value (approx. 2 months free equivalent).

---

## 3. Evaluation & Onboarding

* **14-Day Free Trial**: Full access to all business tier capabilities with zero credit card required upfront.
* **Seamless Transition**: Subscribing converts the trial tenant directly into an active paid subscription with zero data loss or re-configuration.
